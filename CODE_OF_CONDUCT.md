# Community conduct

Human and AI-assisted contributors are welcome. Everyone submitting or reviewing
work remains responsible for its content and for respecting other participants.

- Discuss the work respectfully; offer specific, constructive criticism.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or demean participants.
- Do not publish another person's private information or private-source material.
- Keep Issues and PRs relevant, scoped and free of spam or impersonation.

Human maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation and
apply GitHub's moderation controls proportionately to protect contributors.

For a conduct concern, request a private contact channel through a generic Issue
without naming affected people or publishing incident details. A Human maintainer
must arrange that channel before details are shared. Use GitHub's reporting tools
for platform abuse. No dedicated private inbox is claimed by this document.
