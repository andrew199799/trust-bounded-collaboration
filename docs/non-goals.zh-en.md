# TBC non-goals / TBC 非目标

These boundaries accompany the [vNext positioning](vnext-positioning.zh-en.md). The current executable contract remains `1.0.0`; historical v0.1 material keeps its local-only, mock-only, non-executing scope.

这些边界与 [vNext 定位](vnext-positioning.zh-en.md)配套。当前可执行契约仍为 `1.0.0`；历史 v0.1 材料继续保持仅本地、仅模拟、不执行的范围。

## Reuse the platform floor / 复用平台地板

**TBC is not trying to become the platform beneath every Human-AI organization. It is trying to understand and improve how those organizations actually work on top of increasingly capable platforms.**

**TBC 不试图成为所有 Human-AI 组织脚下的新平台；它关注的是，当底层平台越来越强以后，这些组织究竟怎样才能真正运作得更好。**

TBC does not compete with foundation models, Agent runtimes/orchestration, workflow engines, HITL UI/pause-resume, IAM/OAuth, policy engines, cloud, browser, memory or tool infrastructure and protocols. Reuse mature implementations first. TBC does not build a dashboard, general SDLC or universal governance platform. Organizational principles are not a mandate to implement another infrastructure layer.

TBC 不与大模型、Agent Runtime／编排、工作流引擎、HITL 界面／暂停恢复、IAM／OAuth、策略引擎、云、浏览器、记忆或工具基础设施及协议竞争，优先复用成熟实现。TBC 不建设仪表板、通用 SDLC 或通用治理平台；组织原则不等于要求另建基础设施。

## Human authority and evidence limits / 人的权威与证据边界

**Human-in-command, not Human-in-every-loop.** TBC does not replace Human meaning, judgment, final consequence authority or responsibility. It does not claim to give AI conscience, moral agency or legal responsibility, and is not a substitute for law or institutional governance. Content moderation is not its focus.

**Human 掌握最终指挥权，不必身处每一步循环。** TBC 不替代人的意义、判断、最终后果决定权或责任；不宣称赋予 AI 良知、道德主体性或法律责任，也不替代法律与组织治理。内容审核不是项目重点。

Field-derived cases are sanitized generalizations, not universal validation. Conformance tests establish the evaluator's contract, not product correctness, perfect safety or production readiness for every host. TBC does not claim to have invented AI-native Individual Organizations or that the field is empty. Counterexamples and existing platform solutions can narrow or remove proposed mechanisms.

实践案例是脱敏泛化材料，不代表普遍验证。一致性测试验证评估器契约，不证明产品正确、绝对安全或所有宿主都已具备生产就绪条件。TBC 不声称首创 AI 原生个人组织，也不声称这一领域为空白。反例与现有平台方案可以缩小或消除拟议机制的必要性。

## Executable scope / 可执行范围

The evaluator returns unsigned receipts and never performs the proposed action. Authentication, approval collection, policy decisions, fact verification, replay protection, persistence, live enforcement and recovery remain host responsibilities. Existing demos use synthetic facts offline, without real credentials, funds, live integrations or external side effects. No financial, medical, legal, production, deletion or messaging action is performed by those demos.

评估器返回未签名回执，不实施拟执行动作。认证、批准收集、策略决定、事实验证、重放保护、持久化、真实执行控制与恢复均由宿主负责。现有演示离线使用合成事实，不使用真实凭据、资金、实时集成，也不产生外部副作用；不会实际执行金融、医疗、法律、生产、删除或消息发送动作。

The vNext positioning task does not redesign Core, expand the API, bump the package version, create/move tags, release or publish a package. Repository contribution authority does not authorize a product runtime or live operation. Community participation uses existing Issues and contribution surfaces; no new external platform, service, mailing list, organization or established alliance is implied.

vNext 定位任务不重新设计 Core、不扩展 API、不修改软件包版本、不创建或移动标签，也不发布版本或软件包。仓库贡献授权不等于产品运行时或真实操作授权。社区参与通过现有 Issues 与贡献入口进行，不意味着新增外部平台、服务、邮件列表、组织或已成立的联盟。
